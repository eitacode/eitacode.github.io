About

Software should make the work simpler.

EITACODE started from a simple frustration: most business software is harder to use than the job it's supposed to help with. We build the opposite. Tools that are quick to learn and stay out of your way.

That means getting the basics right. It loads fast, holds up under real use, and stays easy to change a year later when your needs have moved on.

Custom builds
