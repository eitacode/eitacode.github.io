Ordering

How an order runs.

01

Tell us what you need

Garment, quantity, colors, sizes, and the date you need it by. Artwork optional at this stage.

02

We quote it

A written quote with garment options at your quantity, so you can see how the price moves with the count.

03

Approve the artwork

We send a mockup showing placement and size on the actual garment. Nothing goes to print until you sign off.

04

Production and delivery

The run is produced and shipped to you, with the timeline agreed up front on the quote.
